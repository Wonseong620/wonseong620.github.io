Biography

Wonseong Kim is an applied economist working on how institutional language moves markets. His research turns contested public discourse — central-bank statements, food-safety warnings, cross-border media narratives — into measured series, and estimates their effects on prices, trade, and trust.

He holds a PhD in Economics and Statistics (Applied Econometrics) from Korea University (2025) and an MSc in Banking and Finance from the University of St Andrews. Before joining IIASA, he was a visiting research fellow at the Institute of Computer Science (DS-NLP lab), University of St. Gallen, Switzerland. He builds the tools he uses: fine-tuned language models with versioned weights, and LeSentinel, a live news-framing platform running the same pipeline architecture in production.
